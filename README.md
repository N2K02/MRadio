## What you can do

- **Listen to Mauritian radio** — including popular local stations such as Top FM, Radio Plus, Radio One, and MBC stations.
- **Explore worldwide radio** — find stations by name, country, or genre.
- **Save your favourites** — keep the stations you love on Home for quick access.
- **Enjoy uninterrupted listening** — stream in the background with lock-screen, notification, and Bluetooth controls. mRadio can try alternative streams when a station stops working.
- **Listen in your car** — browse and control stations through Android Auto.
- **Make it yours** — use a sleep timer, radio alarm, and custom station links.

The redesigned interface keeps things simple: **Home** for your favourites and player, **Search** for discovering stations, and **Settings** for playback preferences and updates.

## Get started

1. **[Download the latest APK](https://n2k02.github.io/mradio/download/)** from the mRadio download page.
2. Open the downloaded APK on your Android phone and follow the installation prompts.
3. Launch **mRadio**, choose a station, and press Play.

Requires **Android 8.0 or later**. Android may ask you to allow app installation from your browser.

## Updates

Go to **Settings → About & updates** to check for new versions and install them in the app. When an update is available, a **New** badge appears in Settings and on the update menu.

You can also opt into **experimental builds** from the same page. These are intended for testing and may be less stable than official releases.

## Android Auto

mRadio supports Android Auto. Availability of apps installed from APK files can depend on your phone and Android Auto settings. If mRadio does not appear in your launcher, check Android Auto's app visibility and installation restrictions.
